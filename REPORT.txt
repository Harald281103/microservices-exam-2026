communication:
i use rest apis to get and send information to other services that the game needed to run properly
such as auctionhouse checking the players money to see if they can bid or not, like if they have enough money

and i used kafka between score and auctionhouse, as score isnt a neccesity for the game to run as intended.
it also allows the game to continue while score i managing the score
and by using kafka, i can still play the game, even if the leaderboard is temporarily down

i found communication between different microservies to be very straight forward, as clients made it not very hard
and by having these 4 microservies, i could easily organize the work between them

architecture:
i have 4 microservices, and i feel they communicate the way i set up originally
game communicates with auctionhouse and score
it communicates with auction house to help with the flow of the game, and communicate with score to sendt and get scores
